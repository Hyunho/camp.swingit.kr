## 📢 Announcement: CSI 2026 & KLHC 2026

[Notice] Update on CSI 2026 and the Hosting of KLHC 2026

Hello, swing dancers. We are reaching out to share some bittersweet yet hopeful news regarding CSI (Camp Swing It), which many of you have been eagerly awaiting.

As the recovery of the swing scene post-pandemic has been slower than expected, we have made the difficult decision—after careful consideration of current circumstances—not to host CSI at Cheongshim this year. Instead, CSI will take a brief hiatus in 2026 to recharge. In its place, we will focus all our energy on KLHC 2026, which will be held at Kyungsung Hall on the previously announced dates: April 3rd–5th (Fri–Sun).

In 2023, we attempted a similar transition under the name "CSI in Seoul." However, we realized that for many in our community, the identity of CSI is deeply and symbolically tied to the "Cheongshim" venue. To honor this connection and avoid any confusion, we have decided to officially run this year’s event under the title KLHC 2026.

While we won’t be on the vast floor of Cheongshim, we are dedicated to creating an even more intense and passionate competition at Kyungsung Hall—a venue familiar to us all with excellent accessibility. We believe the energy and passion from this year’s KLHC will serve as the foundation for CSI to return to Cheongshim in the future. We kindly ask for your warm support and participation as we take this time to strengthen our roots.

Thank you.
