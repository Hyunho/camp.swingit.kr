<h1 className='not-prose text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight text-gray-900 sm:text-4xl'>Korean Lindy Hop Championships </h1>


Korean Lindy Hop Championships(KLHC) is one of the world’s largest international competitions where the best lindy hoppers for each division are selected. We sincerely hope that KLHC does not stop at just being a place to win simple results but we would like it to be a stage of challenge and opportunity. Let that be you under that spotlight.

Also, due to popular demand from many dancers, we are opening a Balboa competition this year. Don’t miss your chance to win the inaugural title of the most prestigious competition in Korea.


(Korean Lindy Hop Championships(KLHC)는 각 부문별 최고의 린디 하퍼를 선발하는 세계 최대 규모의 국제 대회입니다. KLHC는 단순히 결과만을 받아들이는 행사에 그치지 않기를 진심으로 바라며, 참가자 분들의 도전과 기회의 무대가 되기를 바랍니다. 참여하는 순간 당신이 주인공입니다.

또한 많은 댄서분들의 요청으로 올해에는 발보아 부문 대회도 오픈하게 되었습니다.  대한민국에서 가장 권위있는 대회의 원년 대회 타이틀을 얻을 수 있는 기회를 놓치지 마시기 바랍니다. )

### Routine Divisions

#### Lindy Hop Couple
: A two-to-four minute long routine.

* Price : KRW 60,000/couple (=USD 60)
* Prizes : 1st – Trophy & KRW 300,000 / 2nd – Trophy / 3rd – Trophy (It will be awarded half prize money if less than 10 couples sign up.)

#### Team 
: A two-to-four minute long routine, self-selected music. In teams of more than 3 couples(6 people) and less than 10 couples.(20 people)

* Price : KRW 100,000
* Prizes : 1st – Trophy & KRW 500,000 / 2nd – Trophy / 3rd – Trophy (It will be awarded half prize money if less than 10 teams sign up.)

#### ** All-Star level entry (M&M and Strictly categories)

1. KLHC (CSI) 
    - Advanced category winners (1st-3rd place)
2. The Battle Korea - Final Showdown (excluding regional battle competitions)
    - Advanced Finalist (1st-2nd place)
    - Allstar M&M Winners (1st-3rd place)
3. ILHC
    - Advanced M&M Category Winners (1st-3rd place)
4. Advanced M&M winners of other domestic and international competitions of the same level or higher (1st to 3rd place)

> Until the 2021 competition, qualifications for participation were separated into M&M and Strictly even at the same Advanced level, but since the All-Star section has been established, from 2023, criteria will be given based on level alone without distinction between the two categories.
